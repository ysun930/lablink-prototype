Choose the role that best describes you. Review what information you will provide and how LabLink will use your profile before creating your account.

I am a Student

Create a student research profile that highlights your interests, technical skills, experience, career goals, availability, and preferred type of laboratory position.

Information you will provide

  • Primary research field and sub-discipline
  • Research skills and laboratory techniques
  • Education level and previous experience
  • Career goals and research statement
  • Availability, compensation, and work preferences

What happens next?

  1. Create your LabLink login information.
  2. Complete your student research profile.
  3. LabLink compares your profile with available labs.
  4. Review ranked matches, scores, and explanations.

Your account allows you to save your information, return later, and review previously generated matches.

I am a Lab

Create a laboratory profile that describes your research focus, required techniques, preferred qualifications, career pathways, and student recruitment needs.

Information you will provide

  • Laboratory name, institution, and principal investigator
  • Primary field, sub-disciplines, and laboratory aims
  • Required and preferred research techniques
  • Candidate education and experience preferences
  • Hours, compensation, and work format

What happens next?

  1. Create your LabLink login information.
  2. Complete your laboratory research profile.
  3. Identify the qualifications you need from students.
  4. Make your laboratory available for candidate matching.

Your account allows you to manage your laboratory information and update recruitment requirements later.
