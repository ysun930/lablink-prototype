About LabLink

Smarter Research Matching

LabLink matches student research candidates with laboratories by combining structured profile data with AI-powered semantic analysis, allowing compatibility to be evaluated beyond a single shared keyword.

The Problem

Finding meaningful research opportunities is often difficult for students and early-stage researchers. The usual approach involves cold-emailing faculty members, which can lead to limited responses and provide little insight into what a laboratory is currently studying.


At the same time, research laboratories may struggle to identify candidates with the right academic background, technical skills, research interests, and availability. This gap can limit access to research experience and make the recruitment process less efficient for both sides.

How LabLink Works

Every candidate and laboratory is represented through structured profile information and a detailed written description. These two types of data allow LabLink to evaluate both direct compatibility and broader scientific fit.

1

Structured Matching

Techniques, research fields, sub-disciplines, career goals, availability, and other profile characteristics are compared directly. Each factor can be weighted according to how strongly it indicates compatibility.

2

AI Semantic Matching

The candidate's research statement and the laboratory's description are compared for meaning rather than only identical words. This helps identify related interests even when they are expressed differently.

The AI Layer

The candidate's written research statement is sent to the Cohere Embed API, which converts the text into a numerical representation of its meaning. The same process is applied to each laboratory description. These representations are compared for semantic similarity and blended with the structured profile score.
